METHODOLOGY

How Candidate Scoring Works

Job Match Score = 0.50 × Semantic + 0.30 × Skill + 0.10 × Experience + 0.10 × Education
50%
Semantic Similarity

Dense vector cosine similarity via Sentence-BERT (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) and FAISS inner-product retrieval.

30%
Skill Match Ratio

Intersection between canonical skills in the job description and candidate profile.

10%
Experience Match

Ratio of candidate's verified years of experience to role requirements.

10%
Education Match

Validation of academic degrees (B.Tech, Masters, Computer Science, etc.).
